About me

Download CV View project

I’m a statistics undergraduate at Universitas Padjadjaran in Indonesia. My focus is data science, statistical modeling, and machine learning.

I use Excel, R, Python, and SQL to prepare data and build models. In my currency study, the Indian rupee and the Bhutanese ngultrum turned out to be the rupiah’s closest matches when I compared rates against their five-year and one-month averages.

Where does the rupiah sit among 155 currencies?

I grouped currencies by the cost of one Special Drawing Right relative to each currency’s own history. It took more rupiah than usual to buy one, whether I looked back five years or one month.
