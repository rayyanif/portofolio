About me

Hi, I’m Rayyan Ilyas Fadani, a statistics undergraduate at Universitas Padjadjaran. My interests are in data science, statistical modeling, and machine learning.

I’m interested in how data can help explain real-world problems and support decisions. My projects have taken me from studying exchange rates to modeling health outcomes and designing a library database. Through this work, I prepare data, build models, and explain what the results mean.
